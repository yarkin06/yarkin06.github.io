I am a PhD candidate in Computer Engineering at [Northeastern University](https://www.northeastern.edu/) under the advisory of [Prof. Stratis Ioannidis](https://ece.northeastern.edu/fac-ece/ioannidis/). I have obtained my MS degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from [Northeastern University](https://www.northeastern.edu/) and BS degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from [Bilkent University](https://ee.bilkent.edu.tr/en/).

My research sits at the intersection of machine learning and wireless edge computing, focusing on communication-aware methods for pruning, mapping, and compressing deep neural networks to match hardware topologies, enabling energy-efficient, low-latency distributed inference; and on optimal data selection via Markovian experimental design, keeping models accurate when re-trained under concept drift.

I have a background in developing machine-learning systems across a range of sensing modalities, including Transformer-based wireless radar classification, GPU-accelerated portable RF signal sensing, few-shot sEMG fatigue analysis, and multivariate time-series imputation and forecasting.
