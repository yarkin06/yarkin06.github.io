I am a PhD candidate in Computer Engineering at [Northeastern University](https://www.northeastern.edu/) under the advisory of [Prof. Stratis Ioannidis](https://ece.northeastern.edu/fac-ece/ioannidis/). I have obtained my MS degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from [Northeastern University](https://www.northeastern.edu/) and BS degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from [Bilkent University](https://ee.bilkent.edu.tr/en/).

My research is focused on scalable, communication-aware optimization for pruning, mapping, and compressing deep neural networks aligned with hardware topologies to enable resilient, energy-efficient, and low-latency distributed inference, as well as optimal data selection strategies via Markovian experimental design for re-training machine learning models to maintain predictive performance in the presence of concept drifts.

I have a background on developing machine-learning systems across diverse sensing modalities, including Transformer-based wireless radar classification, GPU-accelerated portable RF signal-sensing, few-shot sEMG motion analysis, and multivariate time-series modeling for imputation and forecasting.
